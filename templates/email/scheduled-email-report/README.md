# Scheduled Email Report

This simple n8n micro-template sends a standard email on a predefined schedule (e.g., weekly).

## Requirements
*   SMTP credentials configured in n8n.

## How to Import
1. Copy the contents of `workflow.json`.
2. In n8n, go to your workspace.
3. Paste the contents directly into the workflow canvas.
4. Set up your SMTP credentials in the Send Email node.
5. Update the sender, recipient, subject, and email body text.
